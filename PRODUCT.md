# Wafa — وفاء

> Keep your word. A clear, interest-free record of money between friends.

**register: product** — design serves the task. Wafa is an authenticated tool people use to track real obligations, so clarity and trust come before spectacle. The brand identity (bilingual, faith-rooted, warm) is expressed through palette, type, and copy, not through decorative UI.

## Product purpose

Wafa records _qard hasan_ (interest-free, goodwill loans) between people who already trust each other. No money moves through the app; it is a shared, auditable ledger of an agreement: requested, approved or countered, transferred, repaid, confirmed. The value is a written record that removes awkwardness, not a payment rail.

## Users

Friends, family, and community members (Gulf/MENA-centred, English-first with Arabic identity) lending each other modest sums (typically tens to low thousands of AED). They are on their phones, mid-conversation, settling a favour. They are not finance professionals and do not want a banking interface; they want something that feels personal, honest, and quick.

## Brand

- **Name:** Wafa (وفاء) — loyalty, faithfulness, keeping a promise.
- **Mark:** a calligraphic Arabic _waw_ (و), coral flowing into deep blue.
- **Personality:** trustworthy, warm, unbureaucratic. A shared notebook between friends, not a bank statement.
- **Voice:** plain, kind, second-person. Short sentences. Never finance jargon, never guilt. "Lend to a friend, written down." "No money moves through Wafa."

## Anti-references

- **Fintech/banking chrome** — navy-and-gold, dense data tables, cold authority. Wafa is interpersonal, not institutional.
- **The emerald/green "money app" reflex** (Robinhood, Cash App). Green-as-primary is the category cliché; Wafa's primary is deep blue, green is reserved only for the _settled_ terminal state.
- **Gamified debt / BNPL energy** — no urgency manipulation, no streaks, no nudging into borrowing.

## Strategic principles

1. **The record is the product.** Every screen reinforces that this is a faithful, shared log, never a place money changes hands.
2. **Interest-free is the identity, not a feature.** Surface "interest-free · qard hasan" in the brand colour, calmly and often.
3. **Status before action.** A person should understand where a loan stands (and whether the ball is in their court) before deciding what to do.
4. **Warm, not cold.** Paper surfaces, real names with avatars, human copy. It should feel like writing something down together.
5. **Mobile-first.** The real moment is two people and a phone. Everything must be excellent at 390px.
